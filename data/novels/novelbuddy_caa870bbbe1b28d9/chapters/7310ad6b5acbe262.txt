1. THERE ARE 2 TYPES OF PLANETS.

FIRST — BARREN PLANET

— A planet which has not become a Path Planet. Its inhabitants are living their lives quietly in the False Universe. After they discover a Path (any supernatural power which evolves its people), their planet will evolve into a Path Planet and will leave the False Universe and enter the True Universe.

For example: Earth is a Barren Planet located in the False Universe.

SECOND — PATH PLANETS

— Path Planets are divided into 3 ranks based on which Zone of the Universe they live in.

1. LESSER PLANET FROM RANK 1 TO RANK 3:

Is present in the Lesser Zone of the True Universe.

2. GREAT PLANET FROM RANK 4 TO RANK 6:

Is present in the Great Zone of the True Universe.

3. HEAVENLY PLANET FROM RANK 7 TO RANK 9:

Is present in the Heavenly Zone of the True Universe.

PATH CREATED BY MAIN CHARACTER SILAS

MENTALIST PATH (WILL PATH):

This Path reaches up to Rank 9 like other Heavenly Paths.

Its first three Ranks are special, as you have to understand your ability. The first ability shows you something about yourself; it also keeps a weakness in you.

The second ability is the most difficult to understand, as you have to guess what your third ability will be to understand it truly. The second ability works as a connection between the first and third abilities.

The first shows the Path, the second becomes the connection between the first and third, and the third becomes the executioner which makes your Will complete and flawless.

It is not important to understand your Will to progress, but if you understand it, your progress will be smooth, and you will achieve a Will without weakness.

RANK 1 (PATH AWAKENED):

Same for almost all the Paths, it focuses on body reconstruction. It is not the same for the Wizard Path.

In the Will Path, the brain is refined in Will Fog, and your goal, emotion, anything you want, or anything you are is added.

For example, a prideful person can form a Prideful Will.

Then they use Will Fog to reconstruct the body, which divides this Rank into four stages like all other Paths.

RANK 2 (SEA MASTER):

Focuses on combining Spiritual Energy with Will. After completely combining both Spiritual Energy and Will, a Sea of Will Power will show signs of crystallization.

RANK 3 (CORE MASTER):

Focuses on adding Soul to the Will Core formed after ascending to Rank 3.

RANK 4 (SOUL SAINT):

Here, all Paths converge and have almost the same power and abilities.

At this level, to progress, you have to understand the Rule which you are most familiar with. It varies from person to person and very much depends upon their experience.

It separates the elite from the noobs.

RANK 5 (RULE SAINT):

At this Rank, the Path Walker has to understand and learn 5 basic Rules of the Universe: Fire, Earth, Water, Wind, and Metal.

RANK 6 (GREAT SAINT):

At this Rank, he will have to assimilate with a Rank 6 weapon which shows the Path to the Heavenly Zone.

RANK 7 (DIVINE SAGE):

Learns and understands the most favored Law. Can also understand the Law of your weapon if your weapon is good.

RANK 8 (HEAVENLY SAGE):

Learns the fundamental Laws of the Universe: the Laws of Water, Earth, Fire, Wind, and Metal.

RANK 9 (HEAVENLY ANCESTOR):

This Rank becomes more difficult the stronger the Law you have learned.

To reach it, you have to surpass the Law you learned and make your own Law, which integrates itself into the Universe, and you become its ruler.
