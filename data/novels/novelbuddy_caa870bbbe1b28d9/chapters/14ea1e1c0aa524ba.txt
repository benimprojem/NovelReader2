Chapter 15: The Path Reaches Rank Nine

Every leader seated around the wooden table had finished their initial brain refinement, yet not one dared to reach for the next step alone. They sat pinned to their chairs, waiting on the boy at the head of the circle.

Men and women who held armies and national treasuries in their palms were sitting like nervous apprentices, waiting for permission to breathe.

A dry smile surfaced on Silas’s face.

"The path you just stepped onto," Silas said, cutting through the heavy air, "has the structural depth to reach Rank Nine, standing level with the Six Heavenly Paths of the cosmos."

The yard went completely still.

Spines straightened around the table.

They might not know the exact geography of the stars, but they understood what Rank Nine meant: the highest ceiling a living being could reach.

Watching the hunger flare in their expressions, Silas felt a flicker of pride. His Mentalist Path was no stolen scrap or cheap derivative. It was an independent doctrine capable of standing against the oldest powers in existence.

He pulled that pride back immediately. The cosmos was littered with forgotten systems. If the existing Heavenly Ancestors had not spent eons stamping out every rising tradition, hundreds of alternative paths would have taken root across history. The road ahead was paved in ash and butchery.

"Before you reach further," Silas held up a single finger, "you need to understand the architecture of this system. The first three ranks of the Mentalist Path work on entirely different rules than traditional cultivation."

The circle leaned inward, hanging on every word.

"You will not just climb by stockpiling raw energy," Silas told them. "Your advancement lives and dies on how deeply you comprehend your own Will."

Glances bounced around the table. Roan broke the quiet first. "What does that comprehension look like in practice?"

"Your first awakened ability will always bring a philosophical revelation with it," Silas said. "The technique itself gives you the power to rebuild your body, but the revelation lays bare your trajectory: your core ambition, your ultimate reason for existing, or the weakness you are destined to tear down."

He paused, letting his gaze turn sharp. "Sometimes, it drags up truths you would rather leave in the dark."

Unease flickered across several faces. Silas lifted a second finger.

"The second ability is considerably harder."

"How so?" George asked.

"Because it cannot be solved on its own," Silas replied. "To unlock the second ability, you have to successfully predict the nature of your future third ability."

The room tightened with focus.

"The first ability sets your course," Silas said. "The second builds the bridge. And the third..."

A brief glint of gold sparked in his irises. "...the third gives your Will the authority to provide you your deepest desire."

Nobody made a sound.

"If you reach full clarity on all three, your cultivation will move with terrifying speed. That said, total enlightenment is not strictly mandatory."

Victoria blinked, surprised.

"You can force your way through the stages by piling on physical refinement," Silas said. "It is slower and far more crude. But if you truly understand what drives you, your Will will reshape reality to match what you want."

Silas opened his right hand. A thick ribbon of golden fog drifted up from his skin, coiling like a serpent over his palm.

"This is where real power begins. Rank One: Path Awakened."

Every pair of eyes locked onto the drifting mist.

"Your brain has already broken down into Will Fog," Silas continued. "Next, you will push that fog through the rest of your physical vessel across four distinct phases."

He ticked them off on his fingers. "First, the skin. Second, the blood. Third, the skeleton. Fourth, the internal organs."

The words burned into their memories.

"Only after the entire physical vessel is remade can you compress the fog inside your skull," Silas said, tapping his temple. "You will pack the mist down until the state of matter shifts entirely."

"Into what?" Qi Feng asked softly.

"A Will Sea."

Silence took the yard again. Silas gave them a few seconds to absorb it before speaking.

"Rank Two: Sea Master. At this stage, your sea of liquid Will must bond with ambient spiritual energy. Once the fluid and the spiritual essence merge, your sea will harden into a crystal lattice and don’t thing you use spiritual energy at rank 1 then you have already bonded with it. You are now using spiritual energy like a fuel but at rank 2 there remains no spiritual energy nor any will fog it merges together to form new higher form of energy."

Silas moved his hand to his sternum.

"That brings you to Rank Three: the Will Core. At this point, your mortal soul enters the structure, locking permanently into the core. The deeper you refine the soul, the greater your command over your strengths."

Silas stopped.

He said nothing about Rank Four. He had already made it clear what happened to people who reached for knowledge they were not ready to carry.

Under the open Indian sky, the future rulers of the planet stared at the child sitting across from them, their old reality washing away like dirt in a storm.

Silas knew every theoretical inch of the road ahead, though in this mortal shell, he still had to walk the path step by step.

He watched the politicians. Their eyes were bright with naked greed.

Good, he thought.

They were exactly what he needed. He had not pulled these particular figures into his backyard on a whim. If he tossed these techniques to average mortals, most would die of old age before parsing the philosophy behind their abilities.

A person’s Will was tempered by the specific weight of their life: their wants, their hatreds, their scars, and the baseline logic they used to survive.

These men and women had spent decades tearing their way to the top of mortal hierarchies. They were apex predators among their kind, which made them ideal subjects for his first trial run. As they whispered among themselves, Silas turned a quieter question over in his head.

Could ordinary people ever decode the Mentalist Path without drowning in the riddles?

His own condition had taken decades of introspection despite a million years of reading cosmic texts in the Grand Library. If every practitioner hit the same wall, the greatest strength of his system could easily turn into its most lethal flaw.

Then again, his own situation was an outlier. His core ambition, total cosmic freedom, was vast enough to swallow star systems. It made sense that his condition was equally convoluted.

What about the rest of them? He studied the table. They would be his baseline. His scouts.

His eyes narrowed. There was another reason he kept the explanation capped at Rank Three: he knew what was waiting outside Earth’s atmospheric shell.

The Lesser Zone was not a place to walk through casually just because one understood theoretical cultivation. It was the most blood-soaked, lawless expanse in the True Universe. In the Greater Zone, conflicts were bounded by imperial treaties, grand alliances, and ancient courts. The Lesser Zone had none of that. It was a meat grinder where even disciples of the Six Heavenly Paths died by the thousands. No single library held a complete star map of the region because the territory was constantly being redrawn by planetary ascensions and butcheries.

Wild, twisted cultivation sects ruled those stars. Where orthodox systems built balanced foundations to prepare for higher realms, practitioners in the Lesser Zone forged broken, front-loaded paths designed purely for immediate murder. There were schools dedicated entirely to soul flaying, puppet stitching, and corpse refinement.

Even worse, wandering among those butchers were ancient horrors that could peel a man’s thoughts apart layer by layer. Entities who commanded esoteric curses Silas had never seen cataloged even in the deepest shelves of the Grand Library.

If those monsters caught the scent of a clean, ninth-rank Path being born on a backwater rock...

Silas’s thoughts went cold.

The secret would never survive. If foreign warlords got their hands on the inheritance of the Mentalist Path, the slaughter would be absolute.

They would not just burn the planet to cinder; they would hunt Silas down to strip the Path Ancestor’s authority from his soul.

Earth would become an ash heap, and every ancient predator in the cosmos would converge on his throat.

Everyone in the Lesser Zone would kill across entire star clusters to claim a path with a ceiling at Rank Nine. Silas looked at the leaders one last time. Rank Three was all they needed to know for now.

The rest of the truth could wait until they were strong enough not to die from hearing it.
