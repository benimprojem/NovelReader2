Chapter 16: Seven Sovereigns, One Year

The first to complete the step was Roan. The moment he did, something about his expression changed. His back seemed straighter, his gaze steadier, and the calmness in his eyes carried a faint sense of superiority.

Silas noticed the change immediately. This was something he already knew will happen. Strengthening ’WILL’ will affect one’s personality but that was not necessarily a bad thing.

In fact, it was a necessary step on every Path, regardless of which Path one followed. Without an anchor, a cultivator who lived for an extremely long time would eventually lose themselves to the endless passage of time.

The mind needed something to hold onto. Something firm which keeps the corruption of time away. That was what the Will provided.

It became an anchor for the mind. Silas examined Roan for a moment before asking,

"What is your Will and Will Ability?"

"My Will is *Above All*."

Roan’s expression became even more prideful as he answered.

"A Prideful Will."

Silas raised an eyebrow.

"And your ability?"

"The more prideful I become, the more I believe in myself, the easier my path become."

Roan fell silent after explaining his ability. Silas became even more interested in the abilities of the others and they didn’t disappoint him.

After all, many of these people had spent years ruling countries and making decisions that affected millions of lives. Their Wills had been tempered by power, responsibility, and ambition. Their paths were clearer than those of ordinary people.

The next to complete the step was Paul. Silas could already tell from his expression what kind of Will he had awakened. His face was tense, his eyes sharp, and there was an unmistakable anger simmering beneath his calm.

A Wrathful Will but Silas still needed to confirm the ability.

"What is your ability?"

"My ability is... the more I get angry, the easier my path become."

Paul’s answer came out more like a growl than a sentence. Silas wasn’t particularly surprised. That had always been Paul’s personality to begin with. The difference was that now, his nature had been brought completely to the surface and transformed into his Will.

The next to complete the step was George. Silas looked at him carefully. He couldn’t immediately determine what kind of Will George had awakened. George’s expression was difficult to read.

His eyes moved calmly as if he were constantly examining everything around him, judging each thing he saw.

What kind of Will is this?

Silas couldn’t wait any longer. He simply asked outright.

"What is your Will and ability?"

"My Will is Justice Will."

George’s expression remained calm.

"And my ability is when I know how to judge, my path will become easier."

He paused after telling his ability. One after another, the leaders began revealing their abilities.

Silas listened carefully to every single one. By now, he had begun to understand the first pattern. Every Will Ability came with a condition. Well, saying it ability can be wrong as first ability is mainly a question or condition like in case of Paul and Roan. They have to embody something. Main power the Will awakened have is their ability to strengthen their body.

Some conditions could remain active almost permanently, such as Pride and Wrath. As long as Roan believed in himself and Paul remained angry, their abilities could continue to make their path easier to walk for them.

Others required an external stimulus. Freedom required someone to force Silas. Justice required George to learn. There were also abilities that granted punishment alongside their power.

Qi Feng, for example, had awakened a **Truthful Will**.

A leader with a Truthful Will was something new to Silas. His ability remained active at all times. His path remains clear until he only speak truth. although he did not have to understand his ability which made it easy for him, but it was not without flaw. Now he cannot lie, if he lied then his path will cut short.

The power it provided was extraordinary, but so was the sacrifice. It could help him, but it also restrains him. Yet the condition itself remained in his hands.

Silas found the entire system increasingly fascinating but now came the true purpose of calling these leaders here. Silas looked at them.

"I will give you all one year."

His voice was calm.

"After one year there should only Seven of you and you will not use any advanced weapon to kill each other. Try to seal all weapon you can seal and leave the one you cannot find to me."

Several leaders stiffened. The leaders exchanged glances. They thought they just have to compete with their ability to get chosen as seven which Silas has mentioned before. But this was a fight to death for seven seats.

"What?"

The word escaped from one of them before he could stop himself.

Silas continued as though he hadn’t heard.

"And after one year, the largest world war in history will begin."

Silence.

Complete silence.

For several seconds, none of the leaders seemed capable of processing what they had just heard. Paul’s eyes widened. George’s calm expression finally cracked. Roan’s eyebrows rose, while Qi Feng stared at Silas as if trying to determine whether he was speaking the truth.

"A world war?" someone finally asked. 𝘧𝘳𝘦ℯ𝓌𝘦𝒷𝘯𝑜𝑣𝘦𝓁.𝒸𝘰𝓂

Silas nodded.

"With only swords and cold weapons."

That made the silence even worse. A few leaders looked at one another, clearly wondering whether they had misunderstood him.

They hadn’t as Silas was completely serious.

"We need to increase the Will of the people. They have lived in peace for far too long."

George immediately leaned forward.

"You can’t possibly"

Silas didn’t give him the opportunity to finish.

"You don’t need to worry about the lives of ordinary people."

The leaders froze.

"In one year, my Life Will Weapon, Earth, will come under my full control."

Silas glanced toward the ground beneath them.

"So, I will establish a rule on Earth. No one will be allowed to die."

The leaders stared at him. Even the most composed among them struggled to hide their disbelief. He was proposing a world war and then casually claiming that he could simply forbid death itself.

"When someone is about to die, they will be sent to a hospital instead."

Silas continued before anyone could interrupt.

"Doctors will not participate in the war. They will remain in hospitals and treat the injured. The people will increase their Will through the war, while the doctors will increase theirs by keeping those people alive."

George’s face tightened.

"That doesn’t make this acceptable."

Silas’s gaze shifted toward him.

"But you will not receive the same protection."

The words immediately silenced him. The leaders understood the implication. Silas wasn’t offering them the same safety he was giving ordinary citizens.

"You see, there cannot be so many rulers on Earth."

His voice remained completely calm.

"So, you will fight against each other until only one ruler remains on each continent."

This time, nobody interrupted. They understood what Silas was asking them to do. They would have to turn against one another. Not with their armies. Not with nuclear weapons. But they will fight personally with each other.

"Keep one thing in mind."
