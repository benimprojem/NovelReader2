Chapter 8: Ch-7.1 The Imperial Guard

While the fleet's operations proceeded seamlessly, Kallus kept himself equally occupied. Utilizing the ship's advanced Deep Sleep Learning Module, he embarked on an ambitious journey to absorb the vast repository of technological knowledge stored within the ship's systems.

The Deep Sleep Learning Module was a marvel of innovation, enabling knowledge assimilation at ten times the normal speed during sleep. Each night, Kallus immersed himself in this accelerated learning process, his subconscious mind tirelessly processing intricate data and complex concepts. The Red Empress monitored his progress closely, providing evening briefings on the ship's operations and ensuring his learning schedule stayed on track.

Despite the system's efficiency, Kallus soon grasped the enormity of the task. After a full month of intensive sessions, he had assimilated only 25% of the database's knowledge. Many technologies were so advanced they felt almost incomprehensible, pushing the boundaries of his intellect.

In addition to his studies, Kallus found himself frequently engaged in meetings with key figures such as Knight Commander Emma and other fleet leaders. These interactions highlighted a pressing need for a clear chain of command to support future expansion. Recognizing the importance of a structured hierarchy, Kallus dedicated time to designing a power structure for his fleet—and eventually, his future empire.

A few days later, during a pivotal meeting, Kallus unveiled the new organizational framework. At its core was the Imperial Guard, led by Knight Grandmaster Emma, who was entrusted with safeguarding him The imperial fleet commander and serving as the cornerstone of his security.

Kallus also established a specialized chain of command for the Imperial Guards, a prestigious and elite unit tasked with his protection and the enforcement of his authority. As the Imperial Commander, Kallus held ultimate control over the Imperial Guards, with the power to appoint their members personally.

At the helm of this formidable force was the Imperial Guard Commander, a position entrusted to Knight Grandmaster Emma. Her leadership was integral to maintaining the unit's discipline, loyalty, and operational effectiveness. The Imperial Guards were not merely bodyguards; they symbolized the might and authority of Kallus's rule, forming a crucial pillar of his expanding empire.

This chain of command ensured that the Imperial Guards operated with precision and unwavering allegiance, embodying Kallus's vision of a structured and secure foundation for his future empire.

Kallus outlined an intricate and hierarchical structure for the Imperial Guards, ensuring both efficiency and clarity in their operations. At the apex of this system stood Kallus himself as the Imperial Commander, with absolute authority over the guards. Supporting him directly was the Imperial Guard Commander, currently Knight Grandmaster Emma, who oversaw all aspects of the unit.

In the future, a Vice Commander role would be introduced, appointed directly by Knight Grandmaster Emma to ensure seamless operations in her absence or during large-scale deployments.

To further enhance their effectiveness, the Imperial Guards were divided into two specialized squads:

The Commander's Guard:

This elite squad was tasked with the personal protection of Kallus. Led directly by Knight Grandmaster Emma, it consisted of:

19 Knight Grandmasters, representing the pinnacle of combat expertise.80 Master Knights, skilled warriors renowned for their discipline and prowess.

The Authority Squad:

This larger force upheld Imperial authority across the fleet and beyond. It comprised 150 Elite Knights, handpicked for their exceptional abilities; 250 Knights, seasoned fighters committed to the empire's ideals; and 500 Apprentice Knights, promising recruits undergoing rigorous training.

This squad was further divided into four units, each capable of operating independently or jointly during critical missions.

This structured approach ensured that the Imperial Guards remained a versatile and formidable force, capable of protecting Kallus while projecting his authority across his growing empire. It also set a clear pathway for promotion and responsibility, fostering loyalty and ambition within their ranks.

Kallus's efforts to create a comprehensive organizational framework for his future empire were ambitious and strategic. The detailed structure he designed for the Imperial Guards reflects his understanding of military and governance principles, recognizing the importance of both security and authority in his quest for expansion. Here is a breakdown of the key elements of his plan:

The Imperial Guard Structure:

Imperial Commander: At the top of the hierarchy, Kallus holds absolute authority over the Imperial Guards, ensuring direct control over the protection and enforcement of his will. His leadership is paramount, as the stability of his rule is tied to the loyalty and effectiveness of his guard.

Imperial Guard Commander

: Knight Grandmaster Emma is entrusted with this crucial role, combining tactical prowess with unwavering loyalty to Kallus. As the head of the guards, she ensures that the Imperial Guards operate smoothly, protecting both Kallus and his broader imperial interests. Her role is central not just in security, but in maintaining discipline and the strength of the unit.

Vice Commander

(Future Role): A position to be introduced in the future, the Vice Commander would be appointed by Knight Grandmaster Emma. This role is designed to ensure continuity and smooth leadership in the event of Emma's absence or when deploying forces on large-scale operations, highlighting the foresight and planning behind Kallus's system.

Strategic Implications:

Hierarchy and Loyalty:

The structured chain of command ensures clarity of roles and accountability. Each level of the guard is designed to operate efficiently, with clear paths for advancement. This fosters loyalty, ambition, and the effective execution of Kallus's commands, which is crucial as his empire expands.

Security and Enforcement:

The dual focus of the Commander's Guard on personal protection and the Authority Squad's broader influence reflects a balance between protecting the emperor and maintaining a strong, visible presence throughout the empire. The latter squad's ability to operate in separate units also means Kallus can maintain power in diverse locations.

Promotion Pathway:

By designing a tiered structure with clearly defined roles and opportunities for advancement, Kallus ensures that the Imperial Guards remain motivated and dedicated to their cause. Knights in each squad are encouraged to strive for higher ranks, creating a merit-based environment.

In sum, Kallus's approach is methodical and forward-thinking, recognizing that a well-organized, disciplined, and loyal guard is integral to his long-term goals of securing and expanding his empire.
