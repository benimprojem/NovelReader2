Chapter 10: Ch-8 The Fleet

During this time, the shipyards were operating at maximum capacity. The fleet had grown exponentially, producing

eight new Antares-Class Battleships

. These ships, while not as advanced as the original two—crafted with Dark Matter-powered systems and enhanced materials—were still formidable.

Each of the new battleships was equipped with:

Antimatter Engines

for powerful propulsion.

Super Light-Speed Drives

, enabling travel at speeds far beyond conventional FTL (faster-than-light) systems.

Although these systems couldn't match the capabilities of the

Dark Matter Generators and Engines

, they were far superior to those used in battleships of similar class across known space.

As Kallus reviewed the new additions to his fleet, a sense of pride and purpose filled him. The Antares-Class Battleships, with their sleek designs and cutting-edge systems, stood as a testament to the progress his crew had achieved in just a month.

He knew the path ahead was still long and fraught with challenges, but with each passing day, his plans gained momentum. The fleet, his knowledge, and his vision were all growing stronger—laying the foundation for the empire he intended to build.

With eight Antares-Class Battleships now operational, in addition to the

Obliterator Dreadnought

and the two original battleships—renamed the

Super Antares-Class Battleships

—Kallus now commanded a fleet of

11 warships

.

He had

15 days left

to complete his mission, and he calculated that the

two internal shipyards

of the Dreadnought could produce

four more Antares-Class Battleships

within that time. That would bring his total fleet to

15 ships

. As the thought of his fleet heading toward Earth flickered in his mind, Kallus shook himself from his daydream. He refocused on the task at hand:

learning and absorbing as much knowledge as possible

in the limited time remaining. He knew that once the deadline passed, his responsibilities would only grow more demanding.

Time flowed relentlessly, and soon

two weeks had passed

. From the

command center

of the Dreadnought, Kallus watched through the

high-quality holographic interface

, which provided a

360-degree external and internal view

of the fleet. The 15th Antares-Class Battleship emerged majestically from the

massive gates

on the lower section of the Dreadnought. As it moved into open space,

its antimatter power generator roared to life

, followed by the activation of its

Super Light-Speed Drive

. The ship smoothly moved into formation, joining the fleet.

Now, the fleet stood fully assembled:

At the

center

was the

Obliterator Dreadnought

, a massive behemoth of power and technology.

At the

lead

were the two

Super Antares-Class Battleships

, their upgraded systems shining as a beacon of Kallus's growing strength.

On either side of the Dreadnought, in perfect symmetry, were the

12 Antares-Class Battleships

,

six on each flank

.

As the final ship took its place, Kallus heard a soft

ding

—the sound of a mission notification. The system revealed that his mission was complete, and the

unknown reward

for the first mission was now visible.

The rewards were:

Supreme God Body

Photographic Memory

Curious and eager, Kallus accepted the rewards. Almost instantly, a surge of

immense energy

engulfed his body. A

barrier

materialized around him, forming a

five-meter protective shield

to contain the chaotic energy and prevent destruction.

What felt like hours passed as Kallus endured the transformation. His body adjusted to the energy coursing through him, every cell rejuvenated and strengthened. When the process finally ended, Kallus felt profoundly different.

He felt His affinity with Dark Matter

had grown exponentially. He could now sense, control, and even absorb it in quantifiable amounts.

His body felt

incredibly powerful

, as if he had transcended mortal limits.

His memory

had become perfect, recalling everything he had learned during his deep sleep sessions with crystal-clear clarity.

Testing his newfound abilities, Kallus spoke aloud, "Red Queen, what's happening to me?"

The Red Queen's voice answered with measured calm, "Master, this is a

complete enhancement of your body

, bestowed by the Supreme Being. However, you must exercise caution. The Supreme Being advises you to familiarize yourself with your new form and refrain from actively absorbing Dark Matter until you complete the next mission. For now, allow your body to absorb it passively."

Kallus nodded, processing her words. Though his newfound power was exhilarating, he knew better than to rush into using abilities he didn't yet fully understand. A faint smile played on his lips as he glanced at the fleet assembled before him.

Kallus adjusted his thoughts and asked, "How much time has passed since I began assimilating the Supreme God Body?"

The Red Queen's calm voice replied, "It has been

five hours

, Master."

Kallus's eyes widened in shock. He sighed and shook his head. "Five hours... That's longer than I expected," he murmured. Turning to the Red Queen, he added, "Have the ship captains, commanders, and other key personnel assemble in the

main hangar

. I have an important announcement to make."

The Red Queen nodded. Closing her eyes briefly, she interfaced with the ship's systems and said, "They have been informed, Master. They will be teleported to the main hangar using the Obliterator's

transportation system

."

Kallus nodded in acknowledgment, marveling once more at the incredible technologies integrated into his flagship by the Supreme Being. The

teleportation system

, along with its countless other features, never ceased to amaze him. Though his

R&D department

worked tirelessly, understanding and replicating these technologies would still take an enormous amount of time and effort.

Pushing these thoughts aside, Kallus leaned back in his command chair, allowing himself a brief moment of calm. Within two or three minutes, a notification arrived, confirming that the preparations were complete. He nodded, stood up, and with a thought,

teleported

directly to the

main hangar

.

There, standing in perfect formation, were the

10,000 crew members

and key personnel of the fleet. Kallus, dressed in his

Fleet Supreme Commander uniform

, stepped onto the raised platform. His commanding presence drew the attention of everyone present.

Clearing his throat, he began, "Everyone, first of all, let me thank you for the

hard work

and

dedication

you've shown over the past month and a half. Your efforts are truly commendable, and for that, you have my utmost respect."

He paused, letting his words sink in before continuing, "The main reason I have gathered you here today is to announce that our

fleet is now complete

. It is time to give it a name—a name that will carry the weight of our ambitions and the legacy we intend to create."

Kallus's voice grew firmer, filled with resolve. "From this moment forward, we shall be known as the

Void Fleet

, and in time, we will establish the

Void Empire

. This fleet is just the beginning. It is the

first and main fleet

, but as we continue to grow and multiply, so too will our might and influence."

A wave of exhilaration swept through the hangar as the crew cheered, their voices echoing in unison. The significance of this moment was not lost on them.

Kallus raised a hand, signaling for silence. "Now," he continued, "in

12 hours

, we will leave this place and embark on the next phase of our journey. I expect every ship to be fully prepared for any eventuality. Ensure that the fleet is in peak condition, and remember: our shipyards will continue to produce ships until we reach our

target of 20 vessels

."

Straightening his posture, Kallus saluted, placing a hand over his chest. "

For the Void Empire!

"

The crew responded in unison, their voices booming with loyalty and determination, "

For the Void Empire!

"

With that, Kallus stepped down from the platform, his heart filled with pride and anticipation. The Void Fleet was ready, and a new Chapter in his journey was about to unfold.
