KONU ÖZETİ
Lin Tian found himself in a cultivation realm after transmigrating, spending two years as a useless member of the Qingyun Sect. After being expelled, he was unable to gather even a single spirit stone. Just as he faced starvation in the wilderness, the Auction System awakened within him. With a hundredfold return, selling a damaged sword would yield a divine weapon, and selling a pill would bring forth a miraculous elixir. While others worried about incurring losses from their shops, Lin Tian was concerned with not earning enough money quickly. Beginning with a crumbling auction house on the brink of closure in the northern part of the city, Lin Tian steadily grew his enterprise across all realms. Those who once belittled him now find themselves kneeling at his auction house's entrance, pleading for him to accept their spirit stones. "What? You think my prices are too high?" "Next, please." [Featuring a story driven by a system and auctions, it’s an invincible and thrilling journey that starts strong, with no suffering for the protagonist—just pure enjoyment!]
